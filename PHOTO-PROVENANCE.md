# Facility photo provenance

This public prototype bundles seventeen media records: eight source-verified records from the official UNIS location directory, retrieved on 2026-09-25, plus nine user-provided photos supplied on 2026-09-27. The Moreno Valley photo is address-matched by visible facade signage. The Houston photo has no visible street-address signage, so its building identity is not independently confirmed from the image. The other seven user-provided photo associations were verified by the users who supplied them and were not independently verified by this prototype. None are official UNIS media, and their original sources and publication rights are not verified. The Tennessee, Las Vegas, and two El Paso sources are Google Maps screenshots with their visible third-party copyright/attribution notices retained in the uncropped detail assets. The images are presentation media only and do not add or alter facility facts.
